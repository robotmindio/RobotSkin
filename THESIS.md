# RobotMind thesis

RobotMind is a modular system for sensors and robotics that children and
professionals can understand and use. It must stay as simple and coherent as
possible while allowing parts to connect by hand and be fixed with screws when
a permanent joint is needed. The assembled system should be as close to
waterproof as practical.

## Minimum first part set

1. **8×8 plate** — the sole structural surface and mounting grid.
2. **Flat join** — joins two plates edge-to-edge.
3. **90° join** — joins two plates at a right angle.
4. **Grove sensor plaque** — mounts a Grove sensor on a plate.

Every future part must reuse this interface. Do not add a new connector,
carrier family, plate size, or enclosure until this four-part set has been
printed and validated.
