# RobotMind assembly

## Hardware

Each occupied lock station uses one M3×3×4, 4.0 mm-OD brass heat-set insert
and one M3×7 pan-head screw. The insert goes into the chamfered centre bore of
the plate's female port. The join or Grove plaque only supplies peg guidance
and a screw-clearance path.

## Sequence

1. For a permanent joint, heat-set one insert in every plate port that will be
   occupied, from the exposed plate face.
2. Press the pegs into the matching plate ports.
3. Drive one M3×7 screw through each occupied lock station into its insert.

Use all 32 lock stations on a full-edge flat or angle join (16 per plate face
on angle joins). The Grove plaque uses its two lock stations. This is the only
defined permanent-lock configuration; press-fit-only assembly remains
removable.
