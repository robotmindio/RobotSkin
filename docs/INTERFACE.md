# RobotMind interface

The plate is 80×80×8 mm with an 8×8, 10 mm-pitch port grid. Port centres are
5 mm from the edges. Both faces are identical.

| Feature | Dimension |
|---|---:|
| Hex port, corner-to-corner | 8.0 mm |
| Hex depth | 2.2 mm |
| Insert pilot / insert | 3.4 mm × 3.0 mm / 4.0 mm OD M3×3×4 |
| Centre membrane | 2.0 mm |
| Peg root / tip | 8.2 / 7.8 mm |
| Peg depth | 2.2 mm |

The 3.4 mm pilot is intentionally smaller than the 4.0 mm insert: heat presses
the brass into the blind bore from the exposed plate face. A hollow peg leaves
clearance for an M3 screw to reach that insert. A pair of pegs stops rotation;
screws make a joint permanent.

Flat joins mount on the plate face across an edge seam. Angle joins mount in
the inside corner: one leg on the floor face and one on the wall face. The
shallow join grooves hold silicone for permanent splash-resistant seams.
Each join uses every one of the eight port columns along its full edge.
