# RobotMind interface

The plate is 80×80×8 mm with an 8×8, 10 mm-pitch port grid. Port centres are
5 mm from the edges. Both faces are identical.

| Feature | Dimension |
|---|---:|
| Annular hex port, corner-to-corner | 8.0 mm |
| Hex depth | 2.2 mm |
| Insert pilot / insert | 3.4 mm × 3.0 mm / 4.0 mm OD M3×3×4 |
| Centre boss / peg clearance | 5.0 / 5.3 mm |
| Centre membrane | 2.0 mm |
| Peg root / tip | 8.2 / 7.8 mm |
| Peg depth | 2.2 mm |

The central boss carries the insert for its full 3 mm depth. Its 3.4 mm pilot
is intentionally smaller than the 4.0 mm insert: heat presses the brass into
the blind bore from the exposed plate face. The peg clears the boss, then its
centre accepts an M3 screw. A pair of pegs stops rotation; screws make a joint
permanent.

Flat joins mount on the plate face across an edge seam. Angle joins mount in
the inside corner: one leg on the floor face and one on the wall face. The
shallow join grooves hold silicone for permanent splash-resistant seams.
Each join uses every one of the eight port columns along its full edge.

`outer_angle_join` mounts below the floor and outside the wall. Its pegs use
the same port dimensions but sit 13 and 23 mm from the exterior corner, rather
than the inside join's 5 and 15 mm rows; the 8 mm shift clears plate thickness.
